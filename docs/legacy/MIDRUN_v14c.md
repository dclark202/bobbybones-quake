everywhere, by a step the pros take: a jump of 269 units that needs about 425 units a second. It is the only big item
on the six maps that nothing but such a step leads to. The item rule, which teaches the intention, named it 22 to 29%
of the time on that map; he went (26% of his intentions), his trips ended 800 units short, and v13 took everything
else. It was in every start of v14 and explains most of the second start's loss on Aerowalk, which I had put down to
the weapon key and the league. The fix (`RULE_WALK=1`, 09:32): the item rule and the item runs count only the ways a
walker can take. You confirmed the cause the same hour ("you need at least a circle jump to get to the red on
aerowalk").
