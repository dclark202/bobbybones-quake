### The knowledge pay and lesson: first start vs v0.3.11b

| | First start | v0.3.11b |
|---|---|---|
| Lesson (readout graded vs truth every frame) | weight 0.3, 10% into his memory, full in 30 min | weight 0.3, **5%** into his memory, full in **3 h** |

- **Lesson** = supervised: "here's the right answer"; changes what he remembers.
- **Pay** = reward: the better-informed side gains what the other loses; changes what he does (listening, checking, staying quiet).

## Long-term plan: one picture of the enemy, used two ways

- The owner: "the fast mechanical network and the strategy network need to share the same representation of the enemy, but act on it
  in different ways." The goal: "truly reactive play (he seems to actually change his intention based on the enemy state)."
- A filter loop: **predict** (where the enemy is and is going) → **update** (sounds, glimpses) → **use** (the hands aim and move; the
  head chooses goals). Built only from his own senses; the truth only grades.
- **v0.4.0**: predict ahead (graded and paid zero-sum; the pay scored every 0.2 s, size × 0.4); the readout's picture of now replaces
  the hand-built belief (last seen place + 1 s of velocity) and the stack filter, as a new input whose gate starts shut; the old guessing
  game removed; the readout kept at export (server work); calibration and grading on the pros' demos (human opponents).
- **v0.4.1**: the prediction fed back too; the small slow head (GRU 256, every 0.2 s, a plan into his fast memory + a bias on his
  intention, both starting at zero) reads the picture, his stack, item timers, score and clock; learns from the two-minute value.
- **Later v0.4.x** (owner: "at least v0.4.1 probably later"): the head takes over the item rule (still at a 10% floor) and the item
  pays; more intentions (contest, wait, stack up, ambush); the head grows only when a decision-report row shows the need.
- v0.4's earlier candidate (~9 M weights, a slow GRU of 1024 and a 3-minute store) is not needed to start: the readout does the
  working-out it was sized for.
