## 2026-10-15 21:25 — The rocket jump check (B-252): as of v0.3 the simulator's rocket jumps are wrong (the blast comes 3 frames late, damage is off by up to 10, jumps are 60 units too low); two switches, both off by default, bring it within 5% and 1 point

**What was done.** `plugins/rjlab.py` (new) makes a bot do scripted rocket jumps on a private server (`set_bot_input`,
three moves a frame). The grid: pitch 45 to 90 by 5 (the game holds 90 at 87.89); the shot on the jump's frame, 1 to 4
frames after it, or standing; no key, forward, strafe, or a 0.6 s run-up; on open floor and by a wall; 100 health with
no armor, 100/100 and 50/25. In all: 243 cases on the test map and 45 on Blood Run, each twice, with every frame
recorded for 2 s. `tools/rj_compare.py` replays the same inputs from the same start in `duel_env.py`.

The two takes of each case match exactly (288 pairs, every number). So the game is deterministic here, and the frame
timing held: 258 cases ran before the v0.3.1 run started at 20:53, and the 15 run-up cases ran beside it.

One snag first. The game's bots join with handicap 50, which is their most health, so their health and armor above 50
fall by one a second. The plugin now sets handicap to 100. The previous helper's 88 records (in `data/rjlab/testlab`)
carry that loss and are not used.

**How the game does it** (fitted from the records and Quake 3's code):
- The rocket leaves on the frame of the command, from the muzzle (eye 26.5 above the origin after the first of the
  frame's three moves, 14 ahead, cut to whole units). At the end of that frame it is 41 units further on. At pitch 65
  and up, standing, it explodes inside that same frame.
- The blast point is cut to whole units toward where the rocket came from.
- Splash damage is 84 x (1 - d / 120), cut to whole points, then halved and cut on oneself. d is measured to his box,
  grown by about 0.5 a side.
- The push is 5 u/s per point of 100 x (1 - d / 120), times 1.1 on oneself and 0.9 on others. A rocket at his own feet
  gives 550 u/s; at another player's feet, 450.
- Armor takes ceil(0.66 x damage), as in Quake 3.
- Quake Live does not cut velocity to whole units after a move: gravity is exactly 20 u/s a frame. A plain jump is
  47.25 high and lands on frame 28.

**Where the simulator of v0.3 differs:**
1. The shot leaves one frame after the command, and the rocket does not move on its first frame. It also starts 40
   units further back. So the blast comes 3 frames late: 0 of 576 jumps had it on the right frame. When he fires on the
   jump's frame, he is 15 units higher by the time it explodes.
2. The splash acts 20 units closer than the box distance (`SPLASH_NEAR`), which makes standing shots too strong. That
   20 came from weaponlab's own aim: it aimed from where it placed the bot, 2 units above where the bot came to rest.
3. The push per point on oneself is 1.3 x 84 = 109.2 (the game's: 110), and its direction is taken from the shifted
   point.
4. Velocity is cut to whole units after every move (`trap_SnapVector` in `sim/sim_api.c`). Gravity therefore loses about
   one unit a frame (19 for the game's 20). Every jump and fall is about 5% higher and longer: a plain jump is 49.67
   high and lands one frame late. **This touches all of his movement, not only rockets.**

**The switches** (in this helper's branch, not merged):
- `RL_QL=1`: the rocket as above.
- `QL_FLOAT=1`: no velocity cut. It needs the library rebuilt (`qsim_snap`, `sim/build.bat`; the Docker image builds its
  own).

With both off the simulator is v0.3's: fixed runs on Blood Run and Aerowalk give the same hash with the old library and
the new one, and `tools/ffa_check.py` reports it identical.
