## 2026-10-24 21:30 — The brain taught on people's games (brainppl): small, real gains on the owner's play tests, self-play held

Branch `brainppl` (from `v042b`), base save: v0.4.2's `keep/pause_160` (update 160). The brain (`belief.*`) and its
readouts (`track.*`, `trackz.*`, `ahead.*`) only; the hands untouched (the saved checkpoint differs in those 18 tensors only).

**Data** (`sim/brain_data.py`; `data/sim_runs/brainppl/data/`): people's 1v1 sessions replayed through today's `observe()`
(sim/head_data.py's Feeder; weapons owned from the pickups; Bobby's logged intention where an input log exists): 205 sessions
listed (24 h of the public archive, 5.5 h of play tests), 137 replayed for the fit (about 21 h); pro demos (the recorder's
senses; the enemy's truth only while the demo sends him): about 7 h in the fit, more building; self-play of save 160 against
itself in whole games, 8 maps x 6 seats x 10 min. Held out: the owner's four v0.4.2 play tests of 2026-10-24 (0.46 h), 15%
of the other sessions by session, 10% of the demos by file, match 0 of every self-play map.

**Labels verified**: a simulator game written as a session and replayed against the simulator's own record (Aerowalk,
Campgrounds, Blood Run; both seats on the last two): on the lesson's frames the place, velocity, zone, speed and way, weapon in
hand, in-view and the race's walking times agree on 99-100% of frames, the 1/2/4 s places within 0.1 unit (median), appear
labels 96-100%, the target proxy 98-100%; weapons owned 67-88% and the mega / red armor timers 70-82% (the writer's pickup
detection and the mega's timer rule; real sessions have the game's pickup events). Inputs: 75-78% of columns equal frame by
frame (the rest is hearing's random draws, his finger limits, the intention's clocks). In whole games the trainer's `done`
is never set at a death (0 resets over every death of the recording): the brain keeps its memory across deaths; the plugin
clears it at a new opponent or game only.

**The target for people** is not known: the next big item (MH, RA, RL, RG, LG, YA, YA2) he picks up within 15 s. A demo holds
only about 40% of the enemy's big pickups (477 against the recorder's 1,229 in the first demos; all of them while the enemy was
in the recorder's PVS): no target labels from demos, and no 1/2/4 s places from demos (graded only where the enemy is sent at
both ends: "hidden enemies come toward me"); demos give the place, zones, speed, way and weapon in hand while he is sent, and
the appearance timing on every frame of a game.

**Domain leak** (`tools/brainppl_leak.py`, one column's AUC against self-play): sessions 24 columns over 0.65 (his own finger
states, frags and score, the enemy seen with rockets, his last death known, wall distances at 22-45 degrees), demos 95. Not
reading them costs much more than it buys: the 24 session columns cost 5-7% of the place error on self-play and the owner's
sessions and the weapons-owned part collapses (0.83 -> 5.7 nats); all 96 columns make the brain useless (place error 130 ->
490 units). Kept them all. The demos improve the held-out demos a lot (place 102 -> 86 units) but not the owner's sessions:
sessions only (`F3`) beat sessions + demos (`F1`) there on every count.

**The fit** (`sim/brain_fit.py`, the trainer's own lesson and weights, lr 5e-5, a pull to save 160's brain 0.3, people 30% of
the lesson, sessions only; GPU, 2 minutes): on the owner's play tests, target top-1 0.244 -> 0.268, its log loss 2.74 -> 2.12,
Brier 0.97 -> 0.89; RL recall 0.06 -> 0.13 (RA 0.54 -> 0.50: RL's row of the confusion has 239 right against 102); P(in view
within 1 s) over the actual rate 1.65 -> 1.38; the 1 s place no better than "he stays where believed" before or after; place
error by time unseen 6-15 s 309 -> 298 units; held-out public sessions place error 178 -> 168. Self-play held out: place error
129.4 -> 131.1 (+1.3%), zones and appearance as before or better. No rise of the target's top-1 from minute 1 to minute 9 of a
game, before or after (in-game adaptation not seen).

**Play effect** (the hands on the owner's recorded inputs, old brain against the tuned one): mean KL 0.0019 a frame, the
intention's most likely choice changed on 0.46% of frames; the intention's shares and the red armor named while down
(0.199) unchanged. Self-play inputs: KL 0.0019. The offline fit alone moves his play very little.
