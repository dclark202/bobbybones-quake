## 2026-10-12 06:55 — "Awaiting snapshot" again on the public server: the seat guard of 2026-10-10 read a field the header has wrong

The owner: "Some tells me the server is stuck on awaiting snapshot again, can you restart it and look into this".
Restarted at 06:47 (`docker restart`), the logs of the hours before kept (`minqlx_20261012_1147_stuck.log`,
`server_20261012_1147_stuck.log` in the server's data folder; the plugin log is there since yesterday's routine, with a
time on every line, and it showed the whole story at once).

**What it was.** The same thing as on 2026-10-10 (RESULTS that day, 20:40), by another road: `!bots 1` on the evening
of the 11th kicked the second Bobby out of seat 1, and from then on every person who connected got seat 1 and no
snapshots: four joins on the morning of the 12th (04:18, 04:19, 06:41, 06:42 PC time), each gone after 36 seconds. The
guard built on the 10th did nothing. It asked whether the seat's client is a bot by the address type in his netchan,
and that field lies in the part of minqlx's `client_t` its own header calls wrong ("everything above this is correct.
Below is a mess"): it read as a bot's for every seat. The check made then, two Bobbys still moving, could not tell: it
shows that a bot's seat is taken for a bot's, never that a person's is not. The case itself (a person in a kicked
Bobby's seat) was left to a play test that did not happen before the next visitor.

**The fix.** `bot_seat()` in `minqlx/botctl.c` now reads the seat's Steam id, the one field below that line the header
has right (minqlx's own player list is made from it, and the plugins know a bot by it): a bot's top twelve bits are
0x014, a person's 0x011. Local server on the new image: both Bobbys driven as before (mean speed 309 and 290). On the
public server only this file was changed (its build otherwise stays the one of 2026-10-11: main has v0.2's code since).

**Still to be seen with a person**: a join into seat 1 after `!bots 1` or `!mode duel`. Until it has been, the claim is
"built on a field known to be right", not "seen working".
