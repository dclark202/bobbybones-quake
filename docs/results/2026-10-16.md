**What the game does that the simulator of v0.3 did not:**
1. **Auto-hop needs the player's own setting.** The server's `pmove_AutoHop 1` re-jumps a held jump on landing only
   for a player whose userinfo carries `cg_autoHop 1`. A person's game sends it (the owner's config has it); a bot's
   userinfo has no such key. With it put into the bot's userinfo the bot hops again; without it every hop chain stopped
   after the first jump. **Bobby on the servers has never auto-hopped, while the simulator he learnt in always did.**
   Fix: `BOBBY_AUTOHOP=1` in `plugins/duelbot.py` and `plugins/ffabot.py` gives every Bobby `cg_autoHop 1` (off by
   default; needs the image rebuilt).
2. **Forward alone pushes past running speed** (`QL_FWD=1`). With the forward (or back) key alone, where Quake 3's push
   gives nothing, the game pushes him along the wish direction by 0.2059 x (640 - his speed) a second: 1.65 u/s a frame
   at 320, 0.72 at 500, 0.2 at 600, nothing at 640 and up (fitted to 0.0001 u/s a frame over 320 to 640). Below 320 with
   the walk key it is the push at 320 (1.65 a frame). The same on the floor, after friction (he slows less with forward
   held), crouched too. With a strafe key held the game is exactly Quake 3. This is what made running rocket jumps and
   short hops with forward held land short (B-252).
3. **The landing** (`QL_LAND=1`). When the floor is met just at the end of a move (a jump from flat floor lands exactly
   on a move's end, since the game does not cut velocity), Quake 3's walk move turned the fall into speed along the
   floor: falling at 400 with 320 along, the simulator came out at 447 and the game at 320 (friction only). The game
   puts the fall's speed to nothing at his first move on the floor.
4. **Jumps** (`QL_JUMP=1`). (a) A jump within 500 ms of the last is faster by 110 x (1 - ms / 500): 125 ms after, 357.5
   against 275, to the game's 0.1; none within 100 ms. (b) A move in the air that ends on a floor higher than it began
   (a stair, a ledge) with the jump held is a jump at once: holding jump up stairs and onto ledges is the game's "double
   jump". (c) Rising in the air he steps onto a ledge (Quake 3 refuses a step while moving up) and keeps the rise; a step
   taken rising costs 3% of his speed along the floor (`pmove_AirStepFriction`).
5. Already known (B-252): velocity cut to whole units after each move (`QL_FLOAT=1`) and the step height of 22
   (`QL_MOVE=1`, of 2026-10-09; not on in v0.3 or v0.3.1).
