# 2.0: pay the stack and the control, not the damage

*2026-11-08 (owner, after watching pro games and playing 1.7a's u90): "we are paying damage far too much. The LG only behavior is likely
because he gets the most pay for using it directly"; "He doesn't value his own life or the positions he holds. He values damage output and
that damage comes from the LG"; "stop trying to change the weapon choice and instead change the incentive"; "Whatever comes next, 1.n is
done; the next round starts with 2.0 and will address the idea of control. Meaning, not giving up your stack for a bit of damage that is
utterly useless. Not revealing your position for no reason. Not barreling past chokepoints"; "I think there's room for experimentation
here, let's really push the boundaries of what we did before." The design and its arms: [design/PLAN_2.md](../../design/PLAN_2.md).*

**The series 2:** combat pay is what a fight does to his chance of winning the game (the frags, the stacks both are left with, the item
control, the clock), never damage by the point. The flat frag and death and the item table by the point stay as the floor (b1's decision).
The weapon is not touched: it is read as an outcome. Fairness limits stay.
