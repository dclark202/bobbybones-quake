# 2.2e: the build's record

Written by the build (an Opus 5.5 worker, 2026-11-19) for the planning session. The card is [CARD.md](CARD.md); this file is what was built,
how it was tested, and where the build decided what the card and the brief left open. Built on `main` (no worktree), not pushed. **The run has
not started**: the planning session starts it with `start.ps1`.

## Read first

1. **The card's premise "the hands are told a drill as they are told a game's kind" is not how the code is: the hands are told no game kind
   today** (no input says whole game, pro start or fight start; INPUTS_22c.csv has none). So the build gives the hands a way of their own: a
   learned vector of 256 (`rdr_emb`, zeros at the start, a parameter of the hands) added to their fast GRU's input on a drill's frames only,
   with its own Adam at the hands' rate. Off, or a frame not in a drill: nothing is added (the network as it was; the checks and servers never
   see a drill and ignore the vector). No input list or network conversion was needed. Shown below (probe: it gets a gradient only from drill
   frames; the trial: its size and gradient every update).
2. **"Distance drawn evenly from 500 to 1,500" is not what the maps allow.** With the aim room's own line finder (a clear line, a lane of at
   least 160 units each way, 96 at 1,500) on a 100-unit grid, most maps have no line beyond 800 or 900 units; only Campgrounds goes to 1,500,
   Furious Heights has one 1,500 line, Lost World and Furious Heights reach 1,100. A uniform draw takes the map's nearest band, so the share of
   placements by band, maps alike: **500-750: 32.5%, 750-1,000: 51.0%, 1,000-1,250: 12.0%, 1,250 and more: 4.5%** (by band: 500 4.5%, 600 10.5%,
   700 17.5%, 800 35.0%, 900 16.0%, 1,000 3.0%, 1,100 8.0%, 1,200-1,400 1% each, 1,500 2.5%). Bloodrun's draws are 85% on its one 700 line.
   A wider spread needs narrower lanes or more maps' long lines; not done (the brief: use the spots that exist).
3. **Only the hands learn from drill frames: proven by a gradient probe**, every parameter group (below). Two leaks were found and fixed on
   the way: the slow plan's rows (inside the item picker) were taken from the drill's *target* seats (scripted, but "his" seats in the
   self-play half), and so could have been the fight network's rows and the tracker's; every non-hands lesson now leaves out both seats of a
   match in a drill.
4. **A NaN found and fixed (a guard, not a design change)**: a learning piece with no learner frame at all gives its advantages' spread of no
   frame (NaN) and poisoned the network in an all-drill CPU smoke (8 updates). The trainer now skips such a piece (counted:
   `rdrill.pieces_skipped`; RDRILL only). In the run's size (28 players a piece, about 70% of them learner seats) it should never happen; a
   non-finite check on the learning's inputs is now in the record and a MAJOR line in the ramp.
5. **2.2d's smoke and full-size trial played without aim2_env.cmd** (the smoke's command is written into the scratch folder, where `call
   %~dp0aim2_env.cmd` finds nothing: the aim2 values at the module's defaults). Technical only (2.2d's run itself was right); fixed in
   2.2e's smoke (the call made absolute), so 2.2e's trial played aim4.
6. **The judge under aim4 accepted by the same test as 2.2d's**: the item picker's flat pay 0.192 of the win part's size (2.2d 0.190), the
   fight network's 0.436 (2.2d 0.395): both scales 1.
7. **The aim room on the exact aim4 default** (Campgrounds, strafe, 8 x 5 min): the lightning gun from 400 (about 313 units) 50.3%, from 200
   (about 193 units) 52.4%: **not over 53%** (`better` reached 53.6% from 200). Table below.
8. The weapon key, the target and its way **learn nothing on drill frames** (they are masked there, the same mask in play and every learning
   pass): the rail is all he has and no item lies there; the drill teaches movement, aim and the trigger. Decided from the card's intent
   ("a hand skill only"); a fork for the planning session if it wants the weapon key in.

### Who learns from drill frames: the gradient probe

`RDR_PROBE` (a test switch): hooks on every parameter of the network, the fight network and the win value, summing each one's gradient over an
update's lessons. "drill": every match in a drill from the first frame (`RDRILL_ALL`) and every non-drill learner frame's weight 0, so what is
measured came from drill frames. "all": ordinary games, no masking (does the probe see the other learners at all?). CPU smokes, 2 simulators x
8 matches; `--brain-human-w 0` (the people's lesson is not this rollout's frames); `SMOKE_CLOCK=20` = past the item picker's freeze and its
warm-up, so its own policy term is live. Each line: tensors reached / tensors in the group.

| Group (what it is) | drill, update 1 (clock 0) | drill, updates 2-6 (clock 20) | all games, updates 1-6 (clock 20) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `enc`, `attn` (31 of 36; 5 are shut tokens), `attn.btok` (the hands' reading of the brain's token), `gru`, `pi`, `v`, `v2`, `legs*` (the hands, their value, their legs) | reached | reached | reached |
| `rdr_emb` (the drill vector) | 1 / 1 | 1 / 1 | 0 (no drill) |
| `aux` (the hands' own forecasts) | 0 | 2 / 10 in updates 2-6 (see below) | 4 / 10 |
| `head2.*` (the item picker: gru, inp, plan, target, trk, v, winp, wsc) | 0 | **0** | reached |
| `FN` (the fight network, the lever) | 0 | **0** | reached |
| `belief`, `track`, `trackz`, `ahead` (the brain and its forecasts) | 0 | **0** | reached |
| `WV` (the win value), `control` | 0 | 0 | WV from update 2; control 0 (its rows need 30 s of horizon) |

**`aux`**: an earlier probe (probe4) showed `aux` reached (2 of 10) on updates 2-6: the hands' forecasts also learn on the previous rollout's
last frames (`--aux-tail`), whose weights were the stored ones; fixed (those weights now leave drill frames out). **The final probe (probe5,
the trainer as committed, clock 20, 6 updates + a resumed one): `aux` 0 in every update; every group but the hands' 0 in updates 1-6.**
In the resumed process's first update `FN` (the fight network) shows 5 of 6 tensors, size 0.171: its forecasts' rows include the first
reading of a new process, which is written before any drill flag reaches the trainer (1,504 of 1,536 rows flagged in the run's own
trial-size count, the other 32 the first reading). In the run this is a game frame (a process starts with no drill until its first round
draw), so not a drill's gradient; but the probe cannot show it from the all-drill side. Not unproven for the run, unproven for that row in
the probe.

The win value learns only states in a game (a drill is not a game) and "in control" is labelled only on ordinary rounds' frames (the
simulator's own rule): neither can take a drill's frame. Before the fixes of "Read first" 3, the clock-20 drill probe showed `head2.plan`
reached (from the target seats' rows).

## Decided in the build (the brief silent; decided from its intent)

| Detail | Decided | Why |
|---|---|---|
| How the hands are told a drill | a learned vector of their own added to their fast GRU's input on drill frames (zeros at the start, own Adam) | no game-kind input exists; this needs no input list or conversion and is off = identical |
| Heads masked on drill frames | the target, its way, the weapon key | "a hand skill only"; nothing to choose there |
| The hands' own forecasts (`--aux`), the teachers | leave drill frames out | forecasts of a script's place teach nothing of a game |
| Who is "the hands" | the encoder, attention (its reading of the brain's token included), the fast GRU, the outputs, the value, the legs' branch and its parts, the drill vector | the network the hands' PPO trains today |
| Distances | uniform draw, the map's nearest band of 100-unit lines | the card's draw on the lines that exist |
| A death in the drill | a new placement at once (counted) | the stretch goes on |
| The target's loadout | the machine gun and the gauntlet (never fired) | the aim room's |
| His stack in the drill | 100 / 100 | the aim room's |
| The share's math | a drill at a round's end with p = S L / (S L + (1 - S) 30 s), L the other rounds' mean length; a match's first round: p = S, begun at a drawn point | the share of the playing time from the first frame, as the other kinds |
| "Fired with the crosshair off the body" | his view on the frame before the shot against the target's box (no world trace) | the aim room's swing measure in the simulator |
| The drill's distance bands for the log | under 500, 500-750, 750-1,000, 1,000-1,250, 1,250 and more (true distance at the shot) | the card's range in quarters |
| Saves | every 10 updates and the end | the card |
| The stop | `--minutes` = the minutes from launch to 08:00 (start.ps1 computes and re-checks them at launch); `--max-updates 1000` | "stops after a save at 08:00" (the trainer stops at the first update's end after its minutes) |
| Checks | 10, 20, 30, then every 10 to 300, 60 also against 2.1d's end, the end; queued | the card; the queue falls about a check behind after 60 |
