# The aim room: 2.2a's update 90 on a fast target (2026-11-19)

Owner, 2026-11-19: "run an experiment with the current model 2.2a u90 with rail in hand in a simulator simple room against a random
moving enemy (fast movement) to track his aim for rail. Then do the same with lg and rockets." It feeds the decision on the aim
model's parameters (aim4). **Measurement only**: nothing here trains; the pros' numbers are a readout of human limits beside his,
never a target or a pass mark. The tool: `tools/run/aim_room.py` (its docstring has the room, the target and every column).

## 1. What he does, in four lines

1. **He holds the weapon and fires.** The tested weapon is in hand 95-100% of his time; the rail at 21-28 shots a minute of the 40
   possible (start 300-1,000 units), the lightning 760-990 cells a minute of 1,200, rockets 31-50 of 75.
2. **He never stands.** 85-90% of his frames are at 200 units a second or more (median 320-330 at the shot), 20-37% in the air,
   22-36 take-offs a minute. Every number here is aim on the move; the harmless target needed no help to show it.
3. **He does not stay.** He closes or leaves: with the rail 95% of the rounds ended because the target had been out of his view
   for a second (a round lasted 2.7 s on average; the target was killed in 1%); with the lightning and rockets 74% (3.4 s; the
   target killed in 19%). Started at 1,000 units his rail shots land at a median of 857; started
   at 1,500, at 941-966 (and he has the target in view only 37% of the time). The far rail is rare because he does not keep the
   distance, not because he does not fire.
4. **The rail's error is about 1.5-2 half body widths where the pros' is 1.0 at every distance; the lightning's equals the pros'
   up to 400 units and doubles beyond 650.** Halving the sight or the hand noise moves the rail by 0-3 points of hits; the
   tracking delay moves it by 15-30 (section 4). **At about 900 units even the delay cut leaves his error at 1.8-2 half bodies**
   (the pros 1.0): the far rail is not limited by the picture's age alone. `max` (every limit off) at 768 units: 41.5% hits,
   1.66 half bodies (base 25.5%, 1.84).

## 2. The room (what was built, and what it is not)

- **Not a test room.** The network reads each map's own tables (the order's map numbers, the map sense) and refuses a map without
  them (`sim/desc22.py need_om`); `testlab`'s rooms cannot seat him. The room is a **line of a duel map**: his spot and the
  target's home in clear sight at the set distance (+-4%), the same floor height (+-40 units), and a lane across the line through
  the target (160-352 units each way) that is walkable and in his sight all along (`aim_room.py spots`, from the maps' way
  points; `data/sim_runs/space/aimroom/spots.json`). **No map has a 1,500-unit line with a 160-unit lane**: at 1,500 the lanes
  are 96-128 units each way (campgrounds 2 lines, furious heights 1), so that target reverses at the lane's end more often.
- Every item held down (taken again at each respawn), dropped weapons removed, his loadout enforced (the weapon, the machine gun,
  the gauntlet; nothing had to be taken away: 0 in every condition). Ammunition refilled every frame; every shot counted.
- A round: 100 health and 100 armor each, he with the weapon in hand, his view on the target's bearing +-45 degrees, his
  sighting reset (the notice delay runs). It ends at 20-30 s, a death, or **the target out of his view for 1 s** (he left).
  His memory is not reset between rounds (a whole-game seat, as in the checks).
- Targets: `strafe` (unarmed; full speed across the lane; reverses 0.3-1.2 s after the last reversal or at the lane's end; holds
  jump in 20% of the stretches: the existing script 1's draws), `dodger` (the same, 0.15-0.5 s), `fighter` (the simulator's own
  script 2, shooting back: the "sniper" persona with the rail, the "dodger" persona with the lightning and rockets; its own
  movement, so its distance is its own). Measured: strafe 318-320 units a second in view, 75 turns a minute; dodger 223-257,
  172 a minute; target in the air 22-54%.
- Script 1 itself cannot be used off a lab map: its moves are relative to a facing that wanders to open space and walks forward
  and back, so it leaves the line. The keys are written by the tool through `duel_eval`'s `BRANCH_HOOK` (no simulator change).

## 3. Settings proof (CLAUDE.md rule 5)

`aim_room.py proof --tag <tag>` compares every job's settings as it ran (its `duel_eval` json) with 2.2d's check json for the
control (`chk_10_prev_bloodrun.json`): `r1` 170 jobs, `r1_far` 12, `sweep` 15; **0 differences not deliberate**. Deliberate:
`OVERTIME 0` (every job), the aim variant's values (process-wide or his seat's, listed per variant), its delays. The same
network file (`control_22a90.pt` = `duel_gru_2_2a/keep/policy_90.pt`, same SHA-1), module `duel_env_ffa`, delays 125 / 225 ms,
his own target lock 8.0, contact 1.0, weapon-own 1 (as `watch.py hold_args`), `docs/INPUTS.csv`. He sits on the even seats only
(the checks swap seats every second process).

Per seat (`SEAT_AIM`, `--aim-he`): PERCEPT_SIGMA, PERCEPT_SPEED, FLINCH_*, MOTOR_NOISE, MOTOR_BASE, SURPRISE_MS, AIM2_W,
AIM2_C_SLIP, AIM2_S0, AIM2_CAP_SLIP, AIM2_C_ECC, AIM2_E0, AIM2_CAP_ECC, AIM2_SPLIT_W, AIM2_FOCUS_GAIN, AIM2_FOCUS_SIG, AIM2_FLINCH,

RANGE_WEBER, PROJ_NOTICE_MS, PROJ_READ_MS, SM_FACE_DEG. The delays are his seats' (`--react-ms`, `--acquire-ms`), in whole

## 6. What could make a number misleading

- **The target is no person**: it runs a lane at full speed and reverses on a random clock; it never closes, never uses cover,
  never stands still. The fighter shoots back but is a script. The lanes are 160-352 units each way (1,500: 96-128).
- **He chooses his fights**: he leaves the target within seconds (most rounds end so); the shots are mostly from the first
  seconds of a sighting. He also closes the distance: the start distance is not the shot's (use the true-distance tables).
- **Items are held down** for the whole room, an unusual state for his item inputs. His memory runs on across rounds and each
  round begins with a teleport (one stale frame).
- **He has trained under none but `base`**: every variant is a readout of what the model's limits cost him as he plays now.
- The delay tau (the frame of the enemy's place set against the view) is a convention: tau 1 for his rail as AIM3.md; at tau 0
  his rail error reads larger. The pros' tau is found per player-game.
- The sweep's cells are one map and 40 match-minutes (700-1,100 rail shots at 1,000 units): about +-3 points of hit share.
- His in-air flag is the simulator's; the pros' "in the air" is the same proxy for both in the split tables (|vertical speed| > 30).
