## Lessons that are now rules

- **Pay**: every pay change states its self-play equilibrium; anything paid for progress is paid on a running best; red-team the
  ledger (negative frags, sign flips, lopsided games) before a go.
- **Starts**: one new thing a run, opened slowly, tied to the checks; a new lesson passes ten updates and a check first.
- **A chooser reads what its choice depends on, at the rate it chooses** (owner, 2026-11-03, from AlphaStar and the b1.2 to
  b1.4 review): no slow-only part for a fast decision. The head alone lost the mega on 9 maps of 10 through a 30 s memory; AlphaStar's
  and Pluto's strategic choices read the present frame. 1.3b meets it for the target (the hands' rows on the fast memory).
- **Hand a choice from a rule to a learner on the learner's own states** (from AlphaStar): a lesson on the frames it plays, faded,
  never copy-then-swap (b1.3a: an 87% copy won 37% alone).
- **The league table** (owner, 2026-11-03, from 1.4b on): his frag share and win chance gained against each league network by
  file, carried from run to run, in the hourly line and every report, beside the checks ([BACKLOG B-300](BACKLOG.md)).
- **Measure, don't guess**: the aim from the pros; calibrations are never skipped or approximated to meet a start time.
- **Settings**: every check and calibration is diffed by script against the run's command, live settings and checkpoint.
- **Data**: never delete near `data`; nightly backups to `T:`.
